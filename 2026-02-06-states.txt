  2. streamingVoicePipeline.ts:91: This callback emits stateChange('idle')

  forceTransitionTo('idle')
  4. olorinVoiceOrchestrator.ts:234-244: Meanwhile, handleContinuation() has a
  fixed 500ms timeout before calling restartListening()

  The race: if audio finishes playing after the 500ms timeout starts
  restartListening(), the onPlaybackComplete callback fires stateChange('idle'),
   which overrides the listening state back to idle.

  The fix: replace the fixed 500ms timeout with event-driven coordination - wait
   for audio playback to complete, then restart listening.
