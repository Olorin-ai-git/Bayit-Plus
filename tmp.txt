⏺ The pipeline is fully wired. Here's the complete flow for "Back to the
  Future":

  ---
  What You'd See

  Prerequisites (data must exist)

  1. Your avatar must be Creatify-ready (status = "ready") with a voice clone
  (elevenlabs_voice_id set, voice_clone_status = "ready")
  2. Interactive characters must be configured for the movie in MongoDB (e.g.,
  Doc Brown, Marty McFly - each with name, voice_id, frame_url, description,
  movie_context)
  3. Interactive moments must exist for the content (at least one, so
  interactionVM gets set and the Talk button appears)
  4. Beta credits - at least 3 per question

  The Flow

  1. Open Back to the Future - Player loads, initializeInteractiveMoments() runs
   in background. Checks preferences, verifies your avatar, loads moments. A
  Talk button (bubble icon) appears in the player controls.
  2. Tap the Talk button - Movie pauses (not ducked).
  PauseAskDialogueOverlayView appears with a dark overlay.
  3. Character selection - You see "Choose who to talk to" with character
  circles (e.g., Doc Brown, Marty) in a grid with pulse animation. Each shows
  the character's frame image + name.
  4. Tap Doc Brown - The overlay creates a VOD interaction session via POST 
  /api/v1/vod-interactions/sessions/start, then transitions to the input panel
  with a text field and Send button (or voice toggle if voice service is
  available).
  5. Type "Doc, will I ever get back to 1985?" and tap Send - Phase transitions
  to "Preparing your question..." with a spinner.
  6. Backend pipeline (~45-85 seconds):
    - Claude Haiku polishes text for grammar/pronunciation (~1s)
    - In parallel: your avatar is animated (ElevenLabs TTS with your cloned
  voice + Aurora lip-sync) AND Claude Sonnet generates Doc Brown's in-character
  response (~20-40s)
    - Then Doc Brown's response is animated (TTS + Aurora lip-sync) (~20-40s)
    - 3 credits charged
  7. User avatar plays - Left circle shows your avatar lip-syncing your
  question. Subtitle reads: "Doc, will I ever get back to 1985?" Right circle
  (Doc Brown) is dimmed at 0.85 scale.
  8. 0.5s transition - Brief pause.
  9. Character response plays - Right circle shows Doc Brown lip-syncing his
  response (in character). Your circle dims. Subtitle shows his response text.
  10. Idle panel - Two buttons: "Ask Another Question" (loops back to input) or
  "Resume Movie" (dismisses overlay, movie resumes playing).

  If you don't have a voice clone

  The same Talk button instead opens the existing AvatarDialogueOverlayView with
   volume ducking (text chat, no lip-sync of your avatar).
