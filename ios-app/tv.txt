⏺ Here's what walkthrough mode (isWalkthrough: true) does for the viewer:

  1. Sets isWalkthroughMode = true on the player state, which flows into the
  Pause & Ask dialogue overlay
  2. Shows a coach mark for 8 seconds when the player opens -- a hint overlay
  telling users they can pause and ask AI questions
  3. Auto-pauses after 15 seconds (VOD only, not live) -- automatically pauses
  playback and triggers the Pause & Ask overlay so the user experiences the AI
  feature without having to discover it themselves
  4. Pre-fills a suggestion in the Pause & Ask input field
  (player.walkthrough.suggestion) so the user can just hit send instead of
  typing
  5. Shows a "works on any content" badge in the Pause & Ask result panel,
  encouraging users to try it on their own content

  In short: walkthrough mode is a guided first-time demo that automatically
  pauses the content and walks the user through the Pause & Ask AI feature,
  lowering the barrier to trying it.

  For Live AI (Channel 13), the auto-pause at 15s is skipped (guard !isLive), so
   the viewer gets the coach mark overlay but the stream keeps playing --
  appropriate since you don't want to pause live TV.

  For Movie AI, the full walkthrough fires: 15 seconds of playback, then
  auto-pause with the pre-filled AI question prompt.
