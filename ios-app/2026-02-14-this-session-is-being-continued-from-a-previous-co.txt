⏺ In-App Purchase Issue - Resolution

  Good news! The app uses Stripe Checkout (external payments), not native iOS
  In-App Purchases. This is allowed by Apple for "reader apps" like Bayit+.

  Current Implementation

  The app creates a Stripe checkout session and opens checkoutUrl in a browser:
  - Backend: /api/v1/subscriptions/checkout creates Stripe session
  - iOS: Opens the checkout URL (external payment)
  - Plans: Basic, Premium, Family (from SUBSCRIPTION_PLANS)

  Apple's Reader App Exemption

  Under Guideline 3.1.3(a), "reader apps" (streaming platforms, magazines,
  newspapers) can use external payment links if they:

  1. Primarily provide previously purchased content ✅ (Bayit+ is a streaming
  platform)
  2. Show clear disclosure before external link ⚠️ (currently missing)
  3. Don't display pricing in-app ⚠️ (we show "$X.XX/mo")

  What We Need to Do

  Option 1: Add Disclosure (Recommended)
  Update SubscriptionView.swift to show a disclosure modal before opening Stripe
   checkout:

  "You will be redirected to our website to complete your subscription. Your 
  subscription will be managed through your Bayit+ account."

  Option 2: Remove Pricing Display
  Remove dollar amounts from the app, use generic language:
  - Instead of: "$9.99/month"
  - Use: "Subscribe to Premium" (show pricing only on Stripe checkout page)

  Option 3: Add "Subscribe on Web" Button
  Instead of direct Stripe checkout, add a button that says:
  "Manage Subscription on bayit.tv"

  Recommendation for App Review Response

  In your App Review response, explain:

  "Bayit+ is a 'reader app' (streaming platform) that uses external payment 
  processing (Stripe) for subscriptions, which is permitted under App Store 
  Review Guideline 3.1.3(a). The app does not use In-App Purchases. We have 
  added the required disclosure before redirecting users to our payment 
  provider."

  Would you like me to:
  1. Implement the disclosure modal before Stripe checkout?
  2. Update pricing display to comply with guidelines?
  3. Both?
