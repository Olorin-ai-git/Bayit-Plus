## For Parents: Getting Started

### What is Zeh Ani?

Zeh Ani ("It's Me" in Hebrew) is an AI-powered feature inside Bayit+ that helps your child connect with Hebrew through personalized content. Instead of passively watching shows, your child becomes part of the story -- their face, their voice, their adventure.

Zeh Ani turns Hebrew from "homework language" into "superpower language."

### How It Works

Zeh Ani has three core experiences:

1. **Star in the Story** -- Your child's 3D avatar appears inside shows, matching each show's art style automatically.
2. **Phonetic Mirror** -- Your child records Hebrew phrases and hears them played back through their avatar with native-level pronunciation, building confidence.
3. **Hebrew Key** -- Interactive moments where the story pauses and your child must speak Hebrew to continue the adventure.

### Step-by-Step Setup

#### Step 1: Open Zeh Ani

From the Bayit+ home screen, tap the **Zeh Ani** section. You will see the Zeh Ani Dashboard showing your child's greeting, highlights, contacts, and feedback.

#### Step 2: Parental Consent (Privacy First)

Before any AI features activate, you must provide parental consent. This is a one-time setup per child profile.

1. Open **Biometric Consent** from the Zeh Ani menu
2. Enter your **4-digit parental PIN** (the same PIN used in Family Controls)
3. You will see three consent toggles:
   - **3D Avatar Generation** -- Allows creating a 3D mesh of your child's face
   - **Voice-to-Voice** -- Allows recording and transforming your child's voice
   - **Advanced AI Features** -- Enables speech-gated narratives and live avatar overlays
4. Toggle on the features you approve
5. Tap **Save**

Your data stays secure:
- All processing uses encrypted connections
- COPPA-compliant data handling
- You can revoke any consent at any time by returning to this screen
- The parental "kill switch" deletes all AI data instantly

#### Step 3: Create the 3D Avatar

Once consent is granted:

1. Open **Avatar Generation**
2. Tap **Generate Avatar**
3. The system creates a personalized 3D mesh of your child
4. You will see a progress indicator: Generating... Rigging... Ready
5. When complete, tap **Preview** to see the 3D avatar
6. You can rotate, zoom, and examine the avatar from all angles

The avatar is stored securely and reused across all Zeh Ani features.

#### Step 4: Set Up Family Contacts

The "Saba/Savta Connection" lets your child share Hebrew progress with family:

1. Open **Contacts** from the Zeh Ani menu
2. Tap **Add Contact**
3. Enter:
   - **Name** (e.g., "Saba Moshe")
   - **Phone number** (with country code)
   - **Relationship** (Grandparent, Parent, Aunt, Uncle, Other)
   - **Preferred language** (Hebrew, English, Spanish, French, Russian, Arabic, Yiddish, German, Portuguese, Italian)
4. Tap **Save**

Contacts receive highlight reels and progress updates via WhatsApp.

#### Step 5: Start Exploring

Your child is now ready to use all Zeh Ani features. Here is what each one does:

### Feature Guide for Parents

#### Magic Mirror

**What it does:** Shows your child's 3D avatar with a personalized Hebrew greeting and vocabulary words of the day.

**What you see:** A 3D animated avatar that waves and speaks, plus Hebrew vocabulary cards showing the word in Hebrew, transliteration (how to pronounce it), and English translation.

**Why it matters:** Daily vocabulary exposure in a fun, personalized context. Your child sees "themselves" as a Hebrew speaker.

#### Voice Practice (Phonetic Mirror)

**What it does:** Your child records themselves saying Hebrew phrases, and the AI plays back a perfected version through their avatar.

**What you see:** A practice screen showing the target Hebrew phrase with transliteration and translation. Your child taps the microphone to record, then sees a before/after score comparison.

**Why it matters:** This bypasses the "shame phase" of language learning. By hearing "themselves" speak with a native accent, children develop confidence and a subconscious identity as a Hebrew speaker.

**Scoring:** Each attempt shows:
- **Before score** -- How the child's raw recording compared to native pronunciation
- **After score** -- How the AI-enhanced version sounds
- **Improvement** -- The delta between the two, showing progress over time

#### Interactive Scenes (Hebrew Key)

**What it does:** During content playback, the story pauses at key moments. Your child must say a Hebrew word or phrase to make the story continue.

**What you see:** A prompt appears showing the target Hebrew word. Your child speaks into the device microphone. If recognized correctly, the story advances. If not, they can try again.

**Why it matters:** Hebrew becomes a "superpower" -- the key to unlocking the next part of the adventure. This transforms language practice from obligation to excitement.

#### Live Avatar Overlay

**What it does:** During content playback, your child's 3D avatar appears as an overlay in the corner of the screen, reacting to the content with facial expressions and lip-sync.

**What you see:** A small 3D avatar in the bottom corner that moves and reacts in real-time via WebSocket connection.

**Why it matters:** Creates a sense of presence and belonging. Your child is not just watching -- they are "in" the show.

#### Highlight Reels

**What it does:** Generates shareable video compilations of your child's Hebrew milestones.

**What you see:** A list of highlight reels with thumbnails, moment counts, and status. You can generate new reels and share them.

**Why it matters:** This is the "Saba Viral Loop." When grandparents in Israel receive a video of their grandchild speaking Hebrew, the emotional impact creates powerful organic word-of-mouth marketing. More importantly, it strengthens family bonds across continents.

**Sharing:** Tap the share button on any ready reel. The link format is `bayit.tv/zeh-ani/reels/{token}` and works via WhatsApp, iMessage, or any messaging app.

#### Feedback Inbox

**What it does:** Displays voice and text messages from family members responding to shared highlight reels.

**What you see:** A list of messages from contacts showing sender name, message preview, and timestamp. Voice messages include a play button for audio playback.

**Why it matters:** Two-way communication completes the feedback loop. When Saba sends back a "Bravo!" voice message, your child feels celebrated for speaking Hebrew.

## Troubleshooting

### Avatar generation stuck on "Generating"
The 3D mesh generation typically takes 30-60 seconds. If it exceeds 2 minutes, close and reopen the Avatar Generation screen. The system polls every 3 seconds and will pick up where it left off.

### Voice recording not working
Ensure Bayit+ has microphone permission in your device Settings. The app records at 16kHz mono PCM for optimal speech recognition quality.

### Highlight reel shows "Processing"
Highlight reel compilation uses FFmpeg server-side and may take 1-2 minutes for longer reels. Refresh the list to check for status updates.

### Contact not receiving WhatsApp messages
Verify the phone number includes the country code (e.g., +972 for Israel, +1 for US). The contact must have WhatsApp installed on that number.

### Live overlay not appearing during playback
The live avatar overlay requires an active WebSocket connection. Ensure you have a stable internet connection. The overlay connects automatically when content starts playing.
