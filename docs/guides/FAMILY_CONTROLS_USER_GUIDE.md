# Family Controls User Guide

**For Parents and Guardians**

**Last Updated**: 2026-02-02
**Platform**: All platforms (Web, iOS, Android, Apple TV)
**Languages**: Available in 10 languages

## What is Family Controls?

Family Controls is Bayit+'s unified parental control system that helps you create a safe viewing experience for your children. With Family Controls, you can:

- **Protect content** with a secure family PIN
- **Set age limits** for kids (0-12) and youngsters (12-17) content
- **Enable or disable** entire content sections
- **Limit content ratings** (G, PG, or PG-13)
- **Set viewing hours** to restrict when content can be watched
- **Manage all platforms** from one central control panel

---

## Quick Start

### 1. Set Up Family Controls

**First Time Setup**:

1. Open Bayit+ on any platform (web, mobile, or TV)
2. Go to **Settings** → **Family Controls**
3. Tap **Set Up Family Controls**
4. Create a **4-6 digit PIN** (remember this!)
5. Choose age limits for kids and youngsters content
6. Tap **Create Controls**

✅ **Done!** Your family controls are now active across all devices.

---

## Features

### 🔒 Family PIN Protection

**What it does**: Requires a PIN to access certain content and change settings.

**When you need it**:
- Accessing youngsters content (ages 12-17)
- Changing family control settings
- Disabling viewing hour restrictions

**How to use**:
1. When prompted, enter your 4-6 digit PIN
2. If you enter it wrong 5 times, your account locks for 15 minutes

**Security Note**: Your PIN is encrypted and never shown in plain text.

---

### 👶 Kids Content (Ages 0-12)

**What it does**: Filters content appropriate for younger children.

**Default Settings**:
- **Age Limit**: 12 years old
- **Content Rating**: G (General Audiences)
- **Enabled**: Yes

**How to adjust**:
1. Go to **Settings** → **Family Controls**
2. Enter your PIN
3. Adjust **Kids Age Limit** (0-12 years)
4. Toggle **Kids Content Enabled** on/off
5. Tap **Save**

---

### 🎓 Youngsters Content (Ages 12-17)

**What it does**: Filters content for teenagers and young adults.

**Default Settings**:
- **Age Limit**: 17 years old
- **Content Rating**: PG-13
- **Enabled**: Yes

**How to adjust**:
1. Go to **Settings** → **Family Controls**
2. Enter your PIN
3. Adjust **Youngsters Age Limit** (12-17 years)
4. Toggle **Youngsters Content Enabled** on/off
5. Tap **Save**

---

### ⭐ Content Rating Limits

**What it does**: Blocks content above a certain rating level.

**Available Ratings** (from least to most restrictive):

| Rating | Description | Examples |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| **G** | General Audiences | All ages appropriate |
| **PG** | Parental Guidance | Some material may not be suitable for children |
| **PG-13** | Parents Strongly Cautioned | Some material may be inappropriate for children under 13 |

**How to set**:
1. Go to **Settings** → **Family Controls**
2. Enter your PIN
3. Select **Maximum Content Rating** (G, PG, or PG-13)
4. Tap **Save**

**Example**: If you set **PG**, then only G and PG content will be accessible. PG-13 and higher will be blocked.

---

### ⏰ Viewing Hours

**What it does**: Limits when content can be watched.

**Use Cases**:
- Prevent late-night watching on school nights
- Enforce bedtime rules
- Limit screen time to specific hours

**How to set up**:
1. Go to **Settings** → **Family Controls**
2. Enter your PIN
3. Toggle **Viewing Hours Enabled** to **ON**
4. Set **Start Hour** (e.g., 8:00 AM)
5. Set **End Hour** (e.g., 8:00 PM)
6. Tap **Save**

**Example**: Start 8:00, End 20:00 means content is only accessible from 8 AM to 8 PM.

**Note**: Times are in 24-hour format (0-23).

## How to Use Family Controls

### Changing Your Settings

**On Web**:
1. Click **Settings** (top right)
2. Click **Family Controls**
3. Enter your PIN when prompted
4. Make your changes
5. Click **Save Changes**

**On Mobile (iOS/Android)**:
1. Tap **Settings** tab
2. Tap **Family Controls**
3. Enter your PIN when prompted
4. Make your changes
5. Tap **Save**

**On Apple TV (tvOS)**:
1. Navigate to **Settings**
2. Select **Family Controls**
3. Enter your PIN using remote
4. Make your changes
5. Select **Save**

---

### Accessing Restricted Content

If content is blocked by family controls, you'll see one of these messages:

- **"This section is disabled by family controls"**
  - The Kids or Youngsters section has been turned off
  - Enter your PIN to temporarily allow access

- **"Content rating too high"**
  - The content exceeds your rating limit
  - Enter your PIN to watch this specific content

- **"Outside viewing hours"**
  - Viewing is restricted to certain hours
  - Enter your PIN to watch now (overrides time restriction)

**To override**: Tap the content → Enter PIN → Watch

---

### Changing Your PIN

**For Security**: Change your PIN regularly or if you think it's been compromised.

**Steps**:
1. Go to **Settings** → **Family Controls**
2. Tap **Change PIN**
3. Enter your **current PIN**
4. Enter your **new PIN** (4-6 digits)
5. Confirm your **new PIN**
6. Tap **Update PIN**

**Important**: You need your current PIN to set a new one. If you enter the current PIN wrong 5 times, your account locks for 15 minutes.

## Troubleshooting

### "Account locked due to too many failed attempts"

**What happened**: You (or someone) entered the wrong PIN 5 times.

**Solution**:
1. Wait 15 minutes
2. The account will automatically unlock
3. Try entering your PIN again

**If you forgot your PIN**:
- Contact Bayit+ support at support@bayitplus.com
- We'll help you reset your family controls

---

### "Family controls not set up"

**What happened**: You haven't created family controls yet.

**Solution**:
1. Go to **Settings** → **Family Controls**
2. Tap **Set Up Family Controls**
3. Create your PIN and settings

---

### Settings don't sync across devices

**What happened**: Family controls should sync instantly, but sometimes there's a delay.

**Solution**:
1. Make sure you're logged in on all devices
2. Close and reopen the Bayit+ app
3. Changes should appear within 30 seconds

**Still not working?**
- Check your internet connection
- Log out and log back in
- Contact support if issue persists

---

### Viewing hours not working correctly

**Possible Issues**:

1. **Time zone mismatch**:
   - Viewing hours use UTC time (universal time)
   - Your device time zone may differ
   - Solution: Set viewing hours based on your local time

2. **Overnight hours** (e.g., 10 PM to 6 AM):
   - Set start hour: 22 (10 PM)
   - Set end hour: 6 (6 AM)
   - System automatically handles overnight ranges

3. **Not blocking content**:
   - Make sure **Viewing Hours Enabled** is toggled ON
   - Check that current time is outside your set hours
   - Try entering PIN to override temporarily

---

### Kids/Youngsters section still showing when disabled

**What happened**: Content may be cached from before you disabled the section.

**Solution**:
1. Close and reopen the app
2. Refresh the page (web)
3. Clear app cache if issue persists

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q: Can I have different PINs for kids and youngsters?

**A:** No, there is one unified family PIN for all content sections. This simplifies management and prevents children from bypassing one PIN to access another section.

---

### Q: What happens if I lose my PIN?

**A:** Contact Bayit+ support at support@bayitplus.com with your account information. We'll verify your identity and help you reset your family controls.

---

### Q: Can my kids reset the PIN if they know my email?

**A:** No. PIN resets require:
1. Your current PIN (to change it)
2. Or support verification (to recover it)

There's no "forgot PIN" option that can be exploited.

---

### Q: Do family controls work offline?

**A:** Partially. The most recent settings are cached locally:
- Kids/Youngsters enable/disable status
- Content rating limits
- Viewing hours

However, PIN changes and new settings require internet connection to sync.

---

### Q: Can I set different rules for different children?

**A:** Not yet. Family controls currently apply to the entire account. Profile-level controls (Phase 4) will allow per-child settings in a future update.

---

### Q: How do I migrate from the old kids/youngsters PIN system?

**A:** Migration is automatic:
1. Log in to Bayit+
2. Go to **Settings** → **Family Controls**
3. Tap **Migrate from Old System**
4. Your existing PIN and settings will transfer
5. You'll now use the unified family controls

---

### Q: Are family controls secure?

**A:** Yes. Security features include:

- **bcrypt encryption**: PINs are hashed, never stored in plain text
- **Account lockout**: 5 failed attempts = 15-minute lockout
- **Rate limiting**: Prevents automated attacks
- **Secure transmission**: All data encrypted in transit (HTTPS)
- **No PIN recovery**: Even we can't see your PIN

---

### Q: Can I disable family controls completely?

**A:** Not currently. Once set up, family controls remain active to ensure child safety. However, you can:

- Enable all sections (Kids and Youngsters)
- Set maximum rating to PG-13 (least restrictive)
- Disable viewing hours

This effectively removes most restrictions while keeping the PIN protection.

## Tips for Parents

### 1. Choose a Strong PIN

- **Don't use**: 1234, 0000, 1111 (too easy to guess)
- **Do use**: Random 4-6 digit combination
- **Keep private**: Don't share your PIN with children

### 2. Test Your Settings

After setting up:
1. Log out
2. Try accessing restricted content
3. Verify restrictions are working as expected

### 3. Adjust as Children Grow

- Review settings every 6 months
- Increase age limits as children mature
- Gradually relax restrictions with demonstrated responsibility

### 4. Use Viewing Hours Strategically

- School nights: Limit to homework hours (e.g., 6 PM - 8 PM)
- Weekends: More flexible (e.g., 8 AM - 10 PM)
- Bedtime enforcement: End hour at your children's bedtime

### 5. Combine with Device Limits

Family controls work best with:
- Device screen time limits (iOS, Android)
- Router parental controls
- Open communication about online safety
