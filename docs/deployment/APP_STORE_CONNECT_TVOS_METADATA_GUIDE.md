## Review Timeline

**Expected Timeline**:
- **In Review**: 24-48 hours after submission
- **Processing**: 1-2 hours (if approved)
- **Available on App Store**: Immediately after processing

**Common Rejection Reasons** (to avoid):
- ❌ Missing demo account credentials
- ❌ Incomplete metadata (screenshots, description)
- ❌ Non-functional features during review
- ❌ Privacy policy issues
- ❌ Age rating inconsistencies

## TestFlight Distribution (While Waiting for Review)

While waiting for App Store review, you can distribute via TestFlight:

1. Go to **TestFlight** tab in App Store Connect
2. Click **External Testing**
3. Create a new group: "Beta 500 Users"
4. Add testers via email or public link
5. Submit build for Beta App Review (faster than full review)
