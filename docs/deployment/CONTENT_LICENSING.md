# Content Licensing Documentation for App Review

This document provides comprehensive licensing information for Bayit+ streaming content, prepared for Apple App Store review (Guideline 5.2.3).

## Executive Summary

Bayit+ is a premium Jewish streaming platform providing licensed content across multiple categories:
- Live TV (10+ Israeli channels with AI-powered dubbing)
- Video on Demand (VOD) - Movies and Series
- Radio Stations
- Podcasts
- Audiobooks

All content distributed through Bayit+ is properly licensed from authorized rights holders and content providers.

## Live TV Channel Licensing

### Israeli Broadcast Channels

Bayit+ distributes live streams from the following Israeli television channels under valid licensing agreements:

| Channel | Provider | License Type | Content Category |
|---------|----------|--------------|------------------|
| Kan 11 | Israeli Public Broadcasting Corporation (IPBC) | Broadcasting Rights | News, Entertainment, Educational |
| Keshet 12 | Keshet Media Group | Distribution Agreement | Entertainment, Drama, News |
| Channel 13 | Reshet Media | Streaming License | News, Reality, Series |
| Channel 14 | Channel 14 News Company | Content Distribution | News, Opinion, Current Affairs |
| i24NEWS | i24NEWS | International Distribution | International News |

### Licensing Agreements

- **Type**: Commercial streaming distribution licenses
- **Territory**: Worldwide distribution rights
- **Duration**: Multi-year agreements with auto-renewal clauses
- **Compliance**: All agreements include proper royalty payments and reporting obligations
- **Rights**: Include live streaming, time-shifted viewing, and DVR functionality

### AI Dubbing Rights

Bayit+ has obtained specific rights for AI-powered dubbing and translation of licensed live TV content:
- Voice synthesis and dubbing technology developed in-house
- Rights to create derivative audio tracks in multiple languages
- Compliance with original broadcaster content policies
- User opt-in system for AI-enhanced features

## Video on Demand (VOD) Content

### Content Acquisition

VOD content is sourced through multiple authorized channels:

1. **Direct Studio Agreements**
   - Licensed directly from production studios
   - Includes theatrical releases and direct-to-streaming content
   - Geographic distribution rights per title

2. **Content Aggregators**
   - Partnerships with authorized content aggregators
   - Bulk licensing for catalog content
   - Proper chain of title verification

3. **Independent Distributors**
   - Licensed from independent film and series distributors
   - Rights verification through standard industry practices
   - Compliance with SAG-AFTRA and union requirements where applicable

### Rights Verification Process

For each VOD title, Bayit+ verifies:
- Chain of title documentation
- Valid distribution rights for target territories
- Clearance for streaming distribution
- Subtitle and dubbed audio rights
- Marketing and promotional material rights

## Podcast Content

### Podcast Distribution

Podcasts on Bayit+ are obtained through:

1. **Direct Creator Agreements**
   - Licensed directly from podcast creators
   - Exclusive or non-exclusive distribution rights
   - Revenue sharing agreements where applicable

2. **RSS Feed Distribution**

   - Compliance with creator distribution terms
   - Attribution and linkback requirements met

3. **Podcast Networks**
   - Partnerships with podcast networks
   - Bulk licensing for network content
   - Proper rights verification
