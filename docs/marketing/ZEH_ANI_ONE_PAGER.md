# Project: "Zeh Ani" (It's Me)

**Last Updated:** 2026-02-12

### The World's First AI-Powered "Identity-Streamer" for the Global Israeli Generation

---

## The Challenge: The "Silent" Second Generation

Millions of Israeli expats in the US face a heartbreaking reality: their children are losing their mother tongue. Despite parents' best efforts, English media (Netflix/YouTube) acts as a cultural "black hole." Hebrew has become a language of "homework and chores," while English is the language of "fun and identity."

---

## The Solution: Moving from "Watching" to "Being"

**Zeh Ani** is not just a streaming app; it is a generative playground. Using 2026's most advanced AI, we don't just show kids Israeli content -- we insert the children **into** the content. We turn Hebrew into the "operating system" of their favorite adventures.

---

## Core Innovation: The "Me-Story" Avatar Engine

Our proprietary AI engine bridges the gap between passive consumption and active fluency through three pillars:

### 1. Visual Immersion (Style-Sync AI)

* **What it is:** A child-safe 3D avatar scan that automatically morphs to match the art style of any show -- from 2D classic animation to 3D CGI.
* **The Impact:** When a child watches *Kofiko*, they aren't watching a monkey; they are *playing* with a monkey in a world where they visually belong.

### 2. The Phonetic Mirror (Confidence-Voice AI)

* **What it is:** We use **Voice-to-Voice AI Synthesis** to record the child's Hebrew and play it back through their avatar -- perfected.
* **The Impact:** By hearing "themselves" speak with a perfect Israeli accent and native-level flow, the child bypasses the "shame phase" of language learning. They develop a subconscious identity as a native Hebrew speaker.

### 3. Narrative Agency (The Hebrew Key)

* **What it is:** AI-driven "Branching Narratives" where the plot only advances if the child interacts in Hebrew.
* **The Impact:** Hebrew is no longer a school subject; it is a "Superpower." To help the hero find the hidden treasure or start the falafel machine, the child must use the language.

---

## Why It Wins (The Business Case)

* **Emotional Retention:** We solve "Parental Guilt." Parents don't just pay for content; they pay for the preservation of their heritage and the connection to the child's grandparents.
* **The "Saba" Viral Loop:** Every session generates a personalized "Hebrew Greeting" video of the child's avatar that is sent to family in Israel via WhatsApp, creating a powerful, organic marketing engine.
* **Unfair Advantage:** While Netflix offers "profiles," we offer "presence." We are building a personalized IP layer that global giants cannot replicate at scale for a niche language market.

## Onboarding: The "Trusted Bridge" Flow

The parent is your **paying customer**, while the child is the **user**. To get the parent to trust an AI-driven app with their child's face and voice, the onboarding must feel less like a "tech setup" and more like a "secure cultural investment."

In 2026, parents are tech-savvy but highly sensitive to data privacy and "AI hallucinations." The following step-by-step onboarding flow is designed to build trust and excitement.

### Step 1: The Emotional Hook (The "Why")

* **Visual:** A short, high-quality montage of an Israeli grandparent receiving a video of their US-based grandchild speaking Hebrew.
* **Text:** *"Hebrew isn't just a language; it's their connection to home. Let's make it their superpower."*
* **Action:** **[Get Started]**

### Step 2: The "Privacy-First" Pledge (The Transparency Layer)

Before asking for any permissions, address the "AI in the room."

* **Visual:** A simple infographic showing a shield over a mobile device.
* **Copy:**

> **"Your Data Stays in the Family."**
>
> * **Local Processing:** Your child's facial and voice scans are processed on *this* device. We don't sell data to third parties.
> * **Closed Loop:** Videos are only shared with the people *you* approve.
> * **Parental Kill-Switch:** Delete all AI data instantly at any time.

* **Action:** **[I Trust This -- Proceed]**

### Step 3: Creating the "Digital Identity" (The Scan)

This needs to feel like a fun activity the parent and child do together.

* **Action:** The parent holds the phone/iPad. The child stands in the frame.
* **The Tech:**
  * **The Face:** A 3D "Circle Scan" (similar to FaceID setup).
  * **The Voice:** The child is asked to say three fun Hebrew words (e.g., *"Bamba," "Shalom," "Sababa"*).
* **The Reveal:** The AI generates the initial Avatar.
* *Prompt to Parent:* *"Look! We've created [Child's Name]'s Israeli twin. This avatar will help them navigate their favorite shows."*

### Step 4: Setting the "Hebrew Dial" (Personalization)

Not every child is at the same level.

* **Interactive Slider:**
  * **Level 1: "The Tourist"** (Mostly English, Hebrew nouns only).
  * **Level 2: "The Expat"** (50/50 mix, focusing on verbs).
  * **Level 3: "The Tzabar"** (Full Hebrew immersion with AI assistance).
* **Goal:** This prevents the child from getting frustrated and quitting on day one.

### Step 5: The "Saba/Savta" Connection (The Viral Loop)

* **Action:** The app asks for permission to connect to WhatsApp or Contacts.
* **Copy:** *"Who should receive [Child's Name]'s Hebrew progress? Add the people who would love a video update from Israel's newest hero."*
* **Input:** Parent adds the grandparents' phone numbers.
