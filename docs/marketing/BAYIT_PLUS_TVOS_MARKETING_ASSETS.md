## 3. Press Release

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

### Bayit+ Launches AI-Powered Israeli Streaming Platform on Apple TV
*Revolutionary app brings real-time translation and cultural context to Israeli television for global diaspora*

**San Francisco, CA - February 4, 2026** — Olorin.ai LLC today announced the launch of Bayit+ for Apple TV, the world's first AI-powered streaming platform dedicated to Israeli and Jewish content. The app introduces groundbreaking features including real-time AI dubbing, smart context cards, and visual sync technology—transforming how millions of people around the world experience Israeli culture.

**THE CHALLENGE**

For decades, Israeli expats, interfaith families, and Hebrew learners have struggled to access Israeli television abroad. Language barriers, missing cultural context, and fragmented streaming services created a frustrating experience that kept people disconnected from the culture they love.

**THE SOLUTION**

Bayit+ solves these challenges by adding an "Intelligence Layer" to streaming. The platform doesn't just deliver content—it translates, explains, and enriches it in real-time.

**KEY INNOVATIONS**

**Real-Time AI Dubbing**
Using advanced speech recognition and neural translation, Bayit+ dubs live Israeli broadcasts (Channels 12, 13, 14) in near-real-time with less than 2 seconds of latency. Users can watch breaking news, reality shows, and dramas in English, Spanish, French, and seven other languages—while the broadcast is still live.

**Smart Context Cards**
Powered by GPT-4 and Olorin.ai's cultural intelligence platform, Bayit+ automatically detects when politicians, historical events, slang, or cultural references appear on screen. Context cards explain exactly who or what is being discussed, turning confusion into connection.

**Visual Sync Technology**
Bayit+'s "Magic Sync" feature allows users to point their iPhone camera at any TV screen playing Israeli content. The app identifies the show and syncs translated audio to the personal device—perfect for families where one person speaks Hebrew and another doesn't.

**Enriched Content Library**
Beyond live TV, Bayit+ offers a curated library of movies, series, podcasts, and audiobooks enhanced with AI-generated summaries, lesson plans for educators, and deep-dive cultural explainers.

**TARGET AUDIENCE**

Bayit+ serves three core audiences:
• **Israeli Expats**: Stay connected to daily Israeli life and news
• **Interfaith Families**: Bridge language gaps and share culture together
• **Educators and Students**: Use immersive content for Hebrew learning and Jewish education

**BETA 500 PROGRAM**

To celebrate the launch, Bayit+ is offering the first 500 users 500 free AI credits to experience all premium features. The Beta 500 program invites early adopters to shape the platform's future through direct feedback.

**PLATFORM AVAILABILITY**

Bayit+ is available on:
• Apple TV (launched today)
• Web (bayit.tv)
• iOS (iPhone and iPad)
• Android (coming Q2 2026)

**TECHNOLOGY STACK**

Built on Olorin.ai's cultural intelligence platform, Bayit+ leverages:
• OpenAI GPT-4 for context generation
• ElevenLabs for text-to-speech synthesis
• MongoDB Atlas for scalable data storage
• Google Cloud for AI/ML infrastructure
• React Native for cross-platform development

**PRICING**

Bayit+ offers a free tier with limited credits and a premium subscription at $14.99/month or $149.99/year. Beta 500 participants receive 500 free credits upon signup.
